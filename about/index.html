About Bjarne

As a student at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa, I speclize in data science and bioinformatics. My research includes: plant genetics, cancer immunogenetics, and bibliometrics.

PhD Fellowship

I am a doctoral student in the department of Molecular Biosciences and Bioengineering. I am a graduate data science fellow for Cyberinfrastructure Training to Advance Climate Science (CI-TRACS). This program aims to build essential CI skills for the Hawaiʻi-Pacific workforce. As a fellow, I helped with the design and implementation of 10 new, publicly available cyberinfrastructure workshops.

Other Things

I enjoy technical work, and managed the 7,000-watt student radio station at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa during graduate school. I played trumpet in the University of Hawaiʻi Jazz Ensemble. I also restore antique radios. I enjoy nature, swimming, hiking, and cycling.

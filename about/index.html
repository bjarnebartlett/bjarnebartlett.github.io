About Bjarne

As a student at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa, I speclize in data science and bioinformatics. My research includes: plant genetics, cancer immunogenetics, and bibliometrics.

PhD Fellowship

I am a graduate student fellow for Cyberinfrastructure Training to Advance Climate Science (CI-TRACS). This program aims to build essential CI skills for the Hawaiʻi-Pacific workforce. As a fellow, I helped with the design and implementation of 10 new, publicly available cyberinfrastructure workshops.

Other Research

My research career began at Johns Hopkins University, where I took a position as a research technologist after receiving my Bachelors of Science in Biology from Gettysburg College. In this position, I became familiar with next generation sequencing technology and began writing biological software. I designed one of the first bioinformatic pipelines to predict cancer neoantigens from whole exome tumor sequencing sequencing data.

Dr. Bjarne Robert Bartlett is an award winning molecular biologist. He is a current postdoctoral scholar in Food Science and Technology at Oregon State University, applying bioinformatic approaches to yeast genomics and smoke taint in wine. He began his scientific career in the field of cancer genetics with Drs. Luis Diaz, Bert Vogelstein, and Ken Kinzler at Johns Hopkins Hospital. Bjarne received his doctoral degree from the University of Hawai’i at Mānoa where he was a graduate fellow with the Hawaiʻi Data Science Institute as a bioinformaticist for plant genomics. He has published research in journals such as Science, The New England Journal of Medicine, and Plants, People, Planet (PPP) across the fields of cancer, plant, and yeast genomcis, as well as data science research for diversity, equity, and inclusion.

In his spare time, he worked as the General Mangaer of KTUH Honolulu, restores antique Atwater Kent tube radios, swimming, hiking, bonding with his rescue chihuahua, and playing jazz trumpet.

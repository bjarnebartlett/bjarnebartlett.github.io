PhD Fellowships


Cyberinfrastructure Training to Advance Climate Science” (CI-TRACS) Fellowship

Bjarne was selected to be a fellow with the Hawaiʻi Data Science Institute as part of a $1 million National Science Foundation grant to increase student cyberinfrastructure skills in climate science.

IDeA Networks of Biomedical Research Excellence (INBRE) Teaching Fellowship

Bjarne was selected to teach bioinformatics as part of the IDeA Networks of Biomedical Research Excellence (INBRE) program. INBRE is a federally funded grant program that encourages undergraduate students to pursue a biomedical science career through a PATHway Activity by providing them with research opportunities, mentoring workshops and workforce development at one of our eight academic institutional partners statewide.

  • KTUH: College Radio Fundraising

    Bjarne Bartlett served as the General Manager at KTUH from 2021-2022. During this time, he expanded the online presence of KTUH by starting a podcast. Dr. Bartlett doubled the fundraising capacity of the organization through unique goals that engaged staff, donors, and alumni. He led the creation of two endowment funds for KTUH to support operational costs and student scholarships.

    Swim Across America: Fundraising for Cancer Research

    Swim Across America, Inc., an organization of volunteer swimmers and friends and family members of cancer patients that raises money for cancer research through swim events. The open water and pool swims have grown and have raised over $3,000,000 in 8 years for the Swim Across America Lab at the Sidney Kimmel Cancer Center at Johns Hopkins.
